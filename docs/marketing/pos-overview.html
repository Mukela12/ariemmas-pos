The System
A complete till, cloud and back-office — in one

TillSync runs on the shop's tills and handles the whole sale — scanning, weighing, receipts, payments and the cash drawer. Every sale is saved instantly on the till and syncs automatically to a secure cloud, so the owner sees live sales and stock from anywhere. It isn't an off-the-shelf app you rent and adapt to — it's our own system, tailored to each business and fully set up, trained and supported.

What it does today
Core features

Everything a busy shop needs to sell fast, track stock and understand its numbers — working reliably whether it's online or off.

Fast checkout

Barcode scanning, instant product search and a clean touch-friendly till screen built for speed.

Weighed products

Integrates with label-printing scales for butchery, produce and deli — scan the weighed label and it rings up.

Works offline

Never stops trading. Sales are safe on the till and upload automatically the moment the connection returns.

Multi-till

Run several registers in one shop, each syncing to a single live dashboard with no duplicate data.

Inventory & stock

Live stock levels, full movement history, low-stock tracking and quick manual adjustments.

Receipts & cash drawer

Thermal receipts (80mm or 58mm, admin-configurable) and automatic cash-drawer opening.

Cashiers & shifts

PIN logins, roles, shift open/close, cash reconciliation and automatic over-cash alerts.

Flexible payments

Cash, mobile money and split payments, with automatic change and tender tracking.

Reports & analytics

Daily sales, revenue, VAT, and breakdowns by cashier and till — with one-click export to Excel.

Cloud dashboard

Owners manage products and watch sales live from any browser — at the shop, at home or on the road.

Product catalog

Categories, product images, pricing and VAT — all managed centrally and pushed to every till.

VAT & tax-ready

Configurable VAT, TPIN printed on receipts, and a clear path to e-invoicing when you need it.

How it works
Offline-first, cloud-connected

The reason it's dependable where other systems struggle: the till never depends on the internet to make a sale.

Tills in the shop

Sell, print and take payment — instantly, with or without internet.

Secure cloud

Sales, stock and settings sync automatically and safely.

Owner's dashboard

Watch sales live and manage the shop from any browser, anywhere.

Every sale is written to the till the instant it happens, so nothing is ever lost — even during a power cut or an internet outage. When the connection returns, the tills and the cloud reconcile automatically, keeping every register and the owner's dashboard perfectly in step.
