Setting Up Products Sold by Weight

Meat, fish, chicken — anything sold by the kilo. Each one needs two steps, and the numbers must match:

⚖️
Part A · The scale
Give the product a number — a PLU — with its name & price per kg.
→
💻
Part B · The POS
Add the product and type that same PLU in “Scale PLU”.
→
🧾
Then · The till
Clerk scans the printed sticker — it rings up on its own.
The one rule The PLU number and the price per kg must be the same on the scale (Part A) and in the POS (Part B). Do both parts for every weighed product.
Example — Beef Sausages, PLU 2, K85/kg Set it on the scale as PLU 2 at K85/kg, then add it in the POS with Scale PLU 2 and price 85. A customer buys 0.620 kg → the sticker scans as Beef Sausages (0.620 kg) = K52.70. Works offline too.
Start your real products at PLU 2 PLU 1 is still the “TEST” item from setup. Either leave it, or reprogram it to a real product using Part A. Give each real product its own number — 2, 3, 4 …
Part A · On the scale

Program the product on the scale

Save, then next or finish The final Amend saves the product — skip it and nothing is saved. To add another, type its PLU number and repeat. When done, press Cancel/Mode to 0.000 kg (or switch off — saved PLUs stay). Write each PLU on the worksheet as you go.
Part B · In the POS

Add the same product in the POS

Do it on a laptop or phone (admin website). It syncs to all three tills. Use the same PLU and price as on the scale.

  1. Open Products → tap “+ Add Product” (or the pencil to edit one).
  2. Enter the Name, pick the Category, set Selling Price = price per kg (same as the scale).
  3. Tick “Sold by weight (butchery)”. The Scale PLU box appears.
  4. Type the Scale PLU — the same number you gave it on the scale.
  5. Tap Save.
  • Name What shows on the screen & receipt.
  • Selling Price Price for one kg. Match the scale.
  • Sold by weight Tick for anything weighed.
  • Scale PLU The product’s number on the scale. Match the scale.

●Check & common mistakes

Test each product Print a sticker from the scale and scan it at a till. It should show the right name, weight and price. If the till says “PLU X isn’t linked,” the numbers don’t match.
Common mistakes • PLU different on the scale vs the POS — must be identical.
• Price per kg different on the scale vs the POS.
• Forgot Amend on the scale, so the product didn’t save.
• Forgot to tick “Sold by weight” in the POS.
• Used PLU 1 for a real product — it’s the TEST item.
