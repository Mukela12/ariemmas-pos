Adding Products Sold by Weight

Meat, fish, chicken — anything sold by the kilo. Set it up in two places, and they must match:

⚖️
1. On the scale
Give the product a number — a PLU — and its price per kg.
→
💻
2. In the POS
Add the product and type that same PLU in “Scale PLU”.
→
🧾
3. At the till
Clerk scans the sticker — it rings up on its own.
The one rule The PLU and the price per kg must be the same on the scale and in the POS.

●Example

Scale: PLU 2 = Beef Sausages, K85/kg.   In the POS: same name, price 85, Scale PLU 2.

Beef Sausages (0.620 kg)  =  K 52.70 The clerk just scans the sticker — no typing. Works offline too.

Do this once per product. Then it works at every till.

●Key it into the POS

Do it on a laptop or phone (admin website). It syncs to all three tills.

  1. Open Products → tap “+ Add Product” (or the pencil to edit one).
  2. Enter the Name, pick the Category, set Selling Price = price per kg.
  3. Tick “Sold by weight (butchery)”. The Scale PLU box appears.
  4. Type the Scale PLU — the same number as on the scale.
  5. Tap Save.

●What each box means

  • Name What shows on the screen & receipt.
  • Selling Price Price for one kg. Match the scale.
  • Sold by weight Tick for anything weighed.
  • Scale PLU The product’s number on the scale. Match the scale.

●Check & common mistakes

Test it Print a sticker and scan it at a till. If it says “PLU X isn’t linked,” the numbers don’t match.
Common mistakes • PLU different on scale vs POS.
• Price per kg different.
• Forgot to tick “Sold by weight”.
• Typed grams, not the price per kg.
